# Privacy Policy for Copy to Sheets

**Effective date:** September 6, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

Copy to Sheets (the “Extension”) exports product information from supported online shopping-cart and order pages into a spreadsheet-ready format.

## Information the Extension Processes

When you click an export button, the Extension may read the following information from the active cart or order page:

- Product names
- SKUs, ASINs, or similar product identifiers
- Product quantities
- Unit prices and subtotals
- Product-page links
- Product-image links
- The active website domain, used to display the appropriate vendor tools

The Extension processes this information locally in your browser to create tab-separated spreadsheet data and Google Sheets formulas.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Extension’s user-facing spreadsheet-export functionality. The generated data is written to your device’s clipboard after you click a copy button so that you can paste it into Google Sheets or another spreadsheet application.

## Data Collection, Storage, and Transmission

Copy to Sheets:

- Does not transmit shopping-cart, order, or browsing information to the developer or to developer-controlled servers.
- Does not store or retain exported information.
- Does not maintain a browsing-history database.
- Does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or behavioral tracking.
- Does not sell, rent, share, or transfer user information to third parties.

All extraction and formatting occur locally on your device.

## Information the Extension Does Not Access

The Extension is not designed to access or collect:

- Payment-card or bank-account numbers
- Passwords or authentication credentials
- Personal communications
- Health information
- Precise location information
- Keystrokes, mouse movements, or general browsing activity

Cart or order pages may display information outside the product area, such as account or shipping details. The Extension’s extraction code targets product information and does not intentionally read those unrelated page sections.

## Permissions

The Extension uses the following browser permissions:

- **`activeTab`** — provides temporary access to the page you have open after you invoke the Extension.
- **`scripting`** — runs the product-extraction function on the active cart or order page after you click a Copy Items button.
- **`clipboardWrite`** — copies headers or formatted product data to your clipboard after you click a copy button.

These permissions are used only for the Extension’s spreadsheet-export purpose.

## Third-Party Services

The Extension does not automatically send information to Google Sheets or any other third-party service. You decide where to paste the exported information. Once you paste information into another service, that service’s terms and privacy policy apply.

Product and image links included in exported data may point to the online store from which the information was extracted. The Extension does not automatically open or transmit data to those links.

## Data Security

Because exported information is processed locally and is not retained by the Extension, the developer does not operate a server-side database containing your cart or order information. You are responsible for protecting the spreadsheet or other destination where you choose to paste the exported data.

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the Extension’s functionality or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
